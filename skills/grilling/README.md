# Grilling

Stress-test a plan or idea before committing to it.

## How to use

```text
Grill me on this plan. Find the assumptions and decisions I haven't thought through.
```

The agent asks questions in rounds, explains its recommendations, and uses your answers to decide what to ask next. When the answer is already in your project, it investigates rather than making you supply it.

Use it to expose gaps, resolve dependent decisions, and reach a shared understanding—not to get an immediate implementation.
